One hill, two moods

From noon the hill is for long lunches: tables in the shade of the pines, canopies in every color, a waterfall you hear before you see it, and room for the kids to run.

  • Kitchen opens at 12 pm
  • Play area for kids
  • Shaded and covered seating

After sunset Amman lights up below you. The cabins glow, the chandeliers come on, and from Wednesday to Friday there is live oud and qanun.

  • Open until 1 am
  • Live music, Wednesday to Friday
  • Decks facing the city lights

Find your corner of the hill

Every space serves lunch, and each one becomes an event venue after dark.

From the kitchen

Jordanian and Levantine cooking: clay pots from the oven, coals lit at the table, sweets made that morning.
